# Invitation requests

Standalone design preview for group invitations in Contacts › Requests: the row in the narrow Contacts list and the detail a click opens in the Contacts main pane, on Mac and iPhone, light and dark. Synthetic people, no network calls. Open `index.html` through a loopback static server.

- Rows: A puts Decline and Join on their own line under the text, B uses small icon buttons, C shows no buttons and answers from the detail.
- Details: 1 leads with the inviter, 2 leads with the group. Both show only who invited you, how (from a room or with a link), the group and its members, and Join or Decline.
